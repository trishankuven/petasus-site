Signing in to your accounts

Petasus keeps each account's sign-in separate in its own workspace. Most accounts sign in exactly as they do in your browser.

What works

  • Your password, plus a code from an authenticator app or a text message (2-step verification).
  • Phone prompts, where you tap Yes on your phone to approve the sign-in.
  • Codes and magic links sent to your email.
  • "Continue with Google" and "Continue with Microsoft" buttons on other sites.
  • Passwords saved in iCloud Keychain or a password manager, when macOS offers them for the page.

What needs passkeys or a security key

  • Google accounts in Google's Advanced Protection Program.
  • Company or school accounts whose sign-in policy requires "phishing-resistant" methods.
  • Accounts where a security key is the only way to complete 2-step verification.

Passkeys and security keys are coming soon.

If a site asks for a passkey or security key

  1. Choose another sign-in method on the site if it offers one (password with a code, or a phone prompt).
  2. Or use "Open in default browser" in the bar Petasus shows, sign in there, and come back.
  3. If your company requires passkeys, ask your administrator which other method is allowed until Petasus supports them.
