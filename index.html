Organized

Separate worlds in one window

Every workspace has its own cookies, logins and site data, kept apart by the operating system itself. Be signed in to your work Gmail, a client's Gmail and your own at the same moment. There is no profile to switch and no wondering which browser you were in.

Need two copies of the same app in one workspace? They get separate sign-ins too. Want two apps to share one login? You can choose that explicitly.

Light

Light enough to leave open

Petasus is built natively on your computer's own web engine, not a bundled copy of Chrome. An app loads when you first open it, goes to sleep when you stop using it, and wakes up where you left it, scroll position included.

Six apps stay awake by default. You choose the number, and you can pin the ones that should never sleep.

In the loop

See what is waiting

Unread counts appear beside each app, on each workspace and on the app icon. Web notifications arrive as ordinary system notifications, and a click takes you to the right app in the right workspace. Mute one app, or set quiet hours for all of them.

Mail apps can optionally show exact counts even while they are asleep.

Private

Quiet by design

Your accounts are yours. Petasus is built so there is as little to share as possible.

Kept apart

Every workspace is sealed

Cookies, logins and site data live in separate storage per workspace, enforced by the operating system rather than by a setting.

On your device

Your data stays here

Your history, cookies and settings stay on your device. Page history is used only for search, and you can turn it off or clear it.

No tracking

Nothing is tracked

There is no analytics. One optional extra, a weekly anonymous performance report, is off until you turn it on, and Petasus shows you exactly what it contains first.

Signed updates

Updates you can trust

Updates are cryptographically signed, and Petasus refuses one that is not. An update check sends the version number and nothing about you.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing for now. If paid plans ever arrive, they will be announced before they start.

Which computers does it run on?

The first release runs on Macs with Apple silicon (macOS 15.4 or later). More platforms are planned.

How is this different from browser profiles?

Profiles also keep accounts apart, but each one is its own window. Petasus puts every account's apps side by side in one window, with unread counts, notifications and search across all of them.

Can it read my email?

No. Petasus shows the sites you open the way a browser does. The optional exact counts for mail use read-only access to label or folder totals, never to messages.

Do passkeys and security keys work?

Not yet. Apple has to approve browser apps for that, and Petasus will use it once it does. Until then you can sign in with a password and a code, or open the page in your default browser. The details are here.
